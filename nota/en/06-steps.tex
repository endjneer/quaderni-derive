\section{Steps made of logarithms}
\label{sec:gradini}

Lines \righe{mate.dfw}{77}{84} build the Heaviside step $H$, the sign
function and some square waves using only exponentials, logarithms and
absolute values. The construction is exact, and the reason is an elementary
lemma.

\begin{lemma}\label{lemma:massimo}
For real $a,b$,
$\dfrac{|a-b|+|a+b|}{2}=\max\bigl(|a|,|b|\bigr)$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
If $|a|\ge|b|$, the numbers $a-b$ and $a+b$ both have the sign of $a$ (or
vanish), so $|a-b|+|a+b|=|2a|$. The case $|b|\ge|a|$ is symmetric.
\end{proof}

With $a=\ee^{x}$ and $b=1$ the lemma gives
$\ln\bigl((|\ee^x-1|+|\ee^x+1|)/2\bigr)=\ln\max(\ee^x,1)=\max(x,0)$, and
therefore, for $x\neq0$,
\[
  \frac{1}{x}\ln\frac{|\ee^x-1|+|\ee^x+1|}{2}=\frac{\max(x,0)}{x}=H(x),
  \qquad
  \frac{2}{x}\ln\frac{|\ee^x-1|+|\ee^x+1|}{2}-1=\sgn x ,
\]
which are lines \#77 and \#81 (line \#80 obtains the sign with $\ee^{2x}$ in
place of $\ee^{x}$). Composing with $\sin\pi x$ gives the square waves of
lines \#82--\#84. Strictly speaking, logarithm and exponential are a detour,
since $H(x)=\max(x,0)/x=(x+|x|)/(2x)$; what the construction really brings
out is that the logarithm turns the maximum of two exponentials into the
maximum of the exponents, $\ln\max(\ee^{a},\ee^{b})=\max(a,b)$.

\paragraph{The logarithm of the hyperbolic cosine.}
The February version of the same notebook records $\ln\cosh x=|x|$
(\righe{mate copia.dfw}{88}{89}). The equality is only asymptotic:
\[
  \ln\cosh x=|x|-\ln2+\ln\bigl(1+\ee^{-2|x|}\bigr),
\]
so $\ln\cosh x-|x|\to-\ln2$ as $|x|\to\infty$, while near zero
$\ln\cosh x\approx x^2/2$. These two lines stand in a precise relation to the
exact construction: $\cosh x=(\ee^{x}+\ee^{-x})/2$ is the \emph{mean} of two
exponentials, whereas $\ee^{|x|}=\max(\ee^{x},\ee^{-x})$ is their
\emph{maximum}.

\paragraph{Context.}
The passage from the mean (or the sum) to the maximum has a name. For every
$h>0$
\[
  \max(a,b)\le h\ln\bigl(\ee^{a/h}+\ee^{b/h}\bigr)\le\max(a,b)+h\ln2 ,
\]
so as $h\to0$ the sum becomes the maximum and, accordingly, the product
becomes the sum. This is \emph{Maslov dequantization}, and the resulting
$(\max,+)$ arithmetic is that of tropical mathematics~\cite{litvinov2007}.
The absolute values of \texttt{mate.dfw} compute the exact maximum, that is
the limiting case $h=0$; the $\ln\cosh$ of \texttt{mate copia.dfw} is the case
$h=1$, with the mean in place of the sum. The same pair appears in neural
networks as $\mathrm{softplus}(x)=\ln(1+\ee^{x})$ and
$\mathrm{ReLU}(x)=\max(x,0)$: the notebooks' step is $\mathrm{ReLU}(x)/x$.

\paragraph{The value of a step at its jump.}
The formula $\max(x,0)/x$ gives no value at $x=0$. A value is obtained only
by choosing a smooth family that converges to the step, and it depends on
the choice. The family
\[
  H_h(x)=\frac{h}{x}\ln\frac{1+\ee^{x/h}}{2},
\]
which is the notebooks' construction with the mean in place of the maximum,
converges to $H$ for $x\neq0$ as $h\to0$ and has limit $\tfrac12$ at $x=0$
for every $h$: this is the usual convention $H(0)=\tfrac12$. But
$\frac12\bigl(1+\tanh(x/h+1)\bigr)$ also converges to $H$ for $x\neq0$, and
at $x=0$ it equals $\frac12(1+\tanh1)=0.8808$ for every $h$.

The notebook \texttt{u(0)-sign(0).dfw} (January 2011) takes up exactly this
question, with four numbers and their differences
(\righe{u(0)-sign(0).dfw}{1}{12}). Lines \#1, \#3, \#5 and \#7 contain the
closed forms: one candidate for the step, $u_1=\ln(\ee-1)=0.5413$, and one for
the sign, $s_2=\ln\sinh1=0.1614$, which line \#3 writes as
$\ln\bigl((\ee^2-1)/(2\ee)\bigr)$; lines \#5 and \#7 carry each into the
other scale through $\sgn=2H-1$, and lines \#9 to \#12 compare the results.
That the second difference is exactly twice the first follows from the
conversion itself. The notebook does not say which family the two
candidates come from, and this cannot be reconstructed with certainty;
neither agrees with the symmetric convention $H(0)=\tfrac12$, $\sgn0=0$.
This is not a mistake: without a family the question has no single answer.

\paragraph{What remains open.}
Nothing mathematical: Maslov dequantization is known theory, and the question
of \texttt{u(0)-sign(0).dfw} has no single answer.
